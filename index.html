Design-basis consistency

Priority valves are source-side: four digester outlets A–D feed the communal header through 8 / 10 / 12 / 14 kPa pressure traps. Household consumers are downstream of purification and do not own those priority valves.

Purifier basis: the final project architecture is simulated as biotrickling/coir → laterite → gravity-fed lime → geo-cool condensate. The supplied cartridge blueprint (iron oxide → laterite → silica gel → activated carbon) is retained as a separately documented mechanical variant, not silently mixed.

Daily demand: the supplied 8.22 Nm³/day target is 0.3425 Nm³/h community average, not 2.05 Nm³/h per household. The simulator scales the supplied four baseline flows to that total.

All unverified plant-sizing numbers are marked in src/core/config.js as simulation assumptions.
